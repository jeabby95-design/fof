About Us

Friends of Fire is a dedicated prayer group bringing together 5 believers committed to intercession, spiritual growth, and community support. We gather regularly to pray for one another, our families, and the world around us.

Our mission is to create a safe, welcoming space where individuals can deepen their faith, strengthen their spiritual foundation, and experience the transformative power of collective prayer. We believe in the unity of purpose and the strength that comes from praying together.

Our Vision

🙏 Spiritual Growth

To foster personal and collective spiritual development through prayer and faith.

🤝 Community Support

To build a loving community where members support each other in prayer and life.

🌍 Global Impact

To intercede for the world and make a positive impact through the power of prayer.
